Paid Search Strategy

Reach the right people at the right moment

Successful PPC advertising is about more than simply turning on a campaign and increasing a daily budget. The keywords you target, the search terms you allow, the message in your ads, the landing page experience, and the way conversions are tracked can all affect campaign performance.

Our Google Ads management approach focuses on building campaigns with a clear purpose. We consider what customers are searching for, what action you want them to take, and how advertising can support your wider digital marketing strategy.

SEO & Paid Search

Paid advertising can work alongside organic SEO

Google Ads and SEO serve different purposes, but they can complement each other. Paid campaigns can provide immediate opportunities to test search demand and messaging, while SEO works toward stronger organic visibility over the longer term.

A coordinated strategy can also help businesses understand which services, keywords, landing pages, and messages deserve more attention. The right mix depends on your goals, market, competition, and available resources.

Frequently Asked Questions

Google Ads questions, answered

What are Google Ads management services?

Google Ads management involves planning, creating, monitoring, and optimizing paid search campaigns to reach relevant audiences and support measurable business objectives.

Can Google Ads help generate leads?

Yes. Google Ads can connect businesses with people actively searching for relevant products and services. Results depend on targeting, competition, offers, landing pages, tracking, and ongoing campaign optimization.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

The right budget depends on your market, competition, goals, customer value, available search demand, and expected conversion economics. A budget should be planned around realistic business objectives.

Do you optimize Google Ads campaigns after launch?

Yes. Campaigns can be refined by reviewing search terms, keywords, advertisements, conversions, targeting, landing pages, and other available performance signals.
